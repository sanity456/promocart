Title
PromotionRuleEngine - Deterministic Category Discount VM

Notes / Description
Built PromotionRuleEngine, a reusable intelligent contract. Its GenLayer-native mechanism is semantic multi-tag plan -> category bitmap -> deterministic priority/exclusivity discount VM. Correction: The validator now recomputes category masks and the tag digest from the attached line-category plan, independently compares the full payload, and revalidates it before writing discount state. Inputs and source references are public, caller-supplied, and unverified. It moves no money, authenticates no merchant, executes no sale, and does not establish price, tax, or consumer-law rights. GenVM lint and strict typecheck pass; 12 direct tests and 1 five-validator integration test pass. StudioNet completed 3 successful isolated-wallet transactions. Its source and full ABI/schema match the pinned evidence exactly. The earlier deployment is superseded.
